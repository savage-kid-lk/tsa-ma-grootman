The Brand

Not defined by age. Defined by mindset.

Tsa Ma Grootman represents a specific kind of man — the modern South African grootman. He carries confidence, experience and self-respect, while still enjoying the finer things in life.

He acknowledges, “ke grootman,” but remains socially active, stylish and connected to culture. He appreciates quality, values presentation and gravitates toward premium experiences.

We're starting with premium-quality T-shirts — with plans to grow as the brand grows.
